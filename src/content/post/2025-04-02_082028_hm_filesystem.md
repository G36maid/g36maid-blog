### Introduction to BTRFS

- BTRFS(B-tree FS) is a modern Linux Filesystem based on Copy-on-Write B-Tree 
- With advanced features: snapshot, subvolume, compression, checksum, etc...
- But the RAID 5/6 modes of Btrfs are **fatally flawed**
- Should not be used for "anything but testing with throw-away data." - Arch wiki


---

### Motivation

- More and more Linux distributions are adopting Btrfs as the default filesystem. 
- Btrfs is also widely used in commercial scenario and NAS solutions like Synology NAS and FNOS.

- However, Btrfs has lack of RAID support, which seems like a drawback.


---

### Main problems of RAID 5/6 in BTRFS

- Write Hole Problem 
    - (How ZFS fixed it?)
- Metadata corruption
    - (Why don't use RAID 5/6 for metadata)
- Lack of Full Parity Consistency 
    - 'btrfs replace' has bugs forcing kernel reboots
    - After an extra reboot may cause real loss.

### Research Goal

1. Survey the implementation of BTRFS RAID 5/6.
2. Analyze how its design flaws are causing the problems.
3. Do some comparison and testing of other filesystems on RAID 5/6.
4. Explore possible solutions, or workarounds with kernel settings adjustments/modules.
