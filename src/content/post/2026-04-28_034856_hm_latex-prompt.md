### 1. 完整最簡範例 (The Cleanest Standard)

> **設計重點**：最基礎、無多餘裝飾的連身乳膠衣。強調一體成型的流暢感。

*(將以下段落插入核心設置中)*

```markdown
She wears a form-fitting black latex bodysuit with a standard round neckline.
The suit is seamless and covers her entire torso and arms.

Her lower body is a seamless, high-gloss black latex mermaid tail that extends smoothly from the bodysuit.
The tail ends in a flexible, semi-transparent organic fin.

```

### 2. 乳膠剪裁選項 (Latex Cut Variations)

> **使用方式**：請用下列任一選項，替換掉上方「完整最簡範例」中的中間兩段（上身與下身描述）。

#### 選項 A：極簡兩件式 (The Minimalist Two-Piece)

> **特點**：抹胸與高腰尾巴的組合，露出腹部。

```markdown
She wears a minimalist black latex bandeau top.
Her midriff is bare, showing her skin between the top and the tail.

Her lower body is a high-gloss black latex mermaid tail that starts high on her waist.
The tail ends in sharp, razor-like mechanical blades.

```

#### 選項 B：側腰幾何挖空 (Geometric Waist Cutouts)

> **特點**：經典的連身衣在腰側進行幾何挖空，增加細節。

```markdown
She wears a form-fitting black latex bodysuit.
The outfit features sharp geometric cutouts on her waist and sides, exposing skin.

Her lower body is a seamless, high-gloss black latex mermaid tail.
The fin is replaced by a silent, rotating magnetic ring drive.

```

#### 選項 C：深 V 領口 (The Deep-V)

> **特點**：強調縱向線條的性感剪裁。

```markdown
She wears a sleek black latex bodysuit.
The design includes a plunging deep-V neckline extending down to her mid-torso.

Her lower body is a seamless, high-gloss black latex mermaid tail.
The tail ends in a flexible, semi-transparent organic fin.

```

#### 選項 D：半高領連身 (The Mock-Neck Unitard)

> **特點**：包覆性高，禁慾且機能感強的剪裁。

```markdown
She wears a mock-neck black latex bodysuit that covers her neck and arms completely.
The silhouette is streamlined and fully covered.

Her lower body is a seamless, high-gloss black latex mermaid tail.
The tail ends in a cluster of vector-thrust ion engines.

```

#### 選項 E：不對稱單袖 (Asymmetrical One-Sleeve)

> **特點**：左右不平衡的設計，增加視覺張力。

```markdown
She wears a form-fitting black latex bodysuit.
It has an asymmetrical design, with the right sleeve fully covered in latex and the left arm completely bare (sleeveless).

Her lower body is a seamless, high-gloss black latex mermaid tail.
The tail ends in sharp, razor-like mechanical blades.

```
