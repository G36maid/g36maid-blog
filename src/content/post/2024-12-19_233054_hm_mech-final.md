> ## problem 10
> 10. Assume that for a given tool and workpiece combination, n = 0.5 and C = 400. Calculate the
> percentage increase in tool life when the cutting speed is reduced by 50%, using the Taylor equation for tool life.

To solve this problem, we will use the Taylor tool life equation, which is given by:

$$ VT^n = C $$

where:
- \( V \) is the cutting speed,
- \( T \) is the tool life,
- \( n \) is the exponent that depends on the tool-workpiece combination,
- \( C \) is a constant that also depends on the tool-workpiece combination.

Given:
- \( n = 0.5 \)
- \( C = 400 \)

Let's denote the initial cutting speed as \( V_1 \) and the initial tool life as \( T_1 \). The Taylor equation for the initial conditions is:

$$ V_1 T_1^{0.5} = 400 $$

Now, the cutting speed is reduced by 50%, so the new cutting speed \( V_2 \) is:

$$ V_2 = 0.5 V_1 $$

We need to find the new tool life \( T_2 \) when the cutting speed is \( V_2 \). Using the Taylor equation for the new conditions:

$$ V_2 T_2^{0.5} = 400 $$

Substitute \( V_2 = 0.5 V_1 \) into the equation:

$$ (0.5 V_1) T_2^{0.5} = 400 $$

Now, we have two equations:

1. \( V_1 T_1^{0.5} = 400 \)
2. \( 0.5 V_1 T_2^{0.5} = 400 \)

Divide the second equation by the first equation to eliminate \( V_1 \):

$$ \frac{0.5 V_1 T_2^{0.5}}{V_1 T_1^{0.5}} = \frac{400}{400} $$

Simplify the equation:

$$ \frac{0.5 T_2^{0.5}}{T_1^{0.5}} = 1 $$

$$ 0.5 \frac{T_2^{0.5}}{T_1^{0.5}} = 1 $$

$$ \frac{T_2^{0.5}}{T_1^{0.5}} = 2 $$

Square both sides to solve for \( T_2 \):

$$ \left( \frac{T_2^{0.5}}{T_1^{0.5}} \right)^2 = 2^2 $$

$$ \frac{T_2}{T_1} = 4 $$

$$ T_2 = 4 T_1 $$

So, the new tool life \( T_2 \) is four times the initial tool life \( T_1 \).

To find the percentage increase in tool life:

$$ \text{Percentage Increase} = \left( \frac{T_2 - T_1}{T_1} \right) \times 100\% $$

Substitute \( T_2 = 4 T_1 \):

$$ \text{Percentage Increase} = \left( \frac{4 T_1 - T_1}{T_1} \right) \times 100\% $$

$$ \text{Percentage Increase} = \left( \frac{3 T_1}{T_1} \right) \times 100\% $$

$$ \text{Percentage Increase} = 3 \times 100\% $$

$$ \text{Percentage Increase} = 300\% $$

Therefore, the percentage increase in tool life when the cutting speed is reduced by 50% is 300%.

## Given Data:
- Diameter of the cutter (D) = 150
- Width of cut (W) = 60 mm
- Length of cut (l) = 500 mm
- Depth of cut (d) = 3 mm
- Cutting speed (v) = 0.6 m/min
- Spindle speed (N) = 1000 rpm
- Number of inserts (z) = 10
- Workpiece material: High-strength aluminum alloy

### Calculations:

#### 1. Feed per tooth (f_z):
The feed per tooth can be calculated using the formula:
$$ f_z = \frac{v}{N \times z} $$

Where:
- \( v \) is the cutting speed in mm/min (convert from m/min to mm/min by multiplying by 1000)
- \( N \) is the spindle speed in rpm
- \( z \) is the number of inserts

$$ v = 0.6 \, \text{m/min} \times 1000 = 600 \, \text{mm/min} $$

$$ f_z = \frac{600 \, \text{mm/min}}{1000 \, \text{rpm} \times 10} = 0.06 \, \text{mm/tooth} $$

#### 2. Metal Removal Rate (MRR):
The metal removal rate can be calculated using the formula:
$$ \text{MRR} = W \times d \times v $$

Where:
- \( W \) is the width of cut in mm
- \( d \) is the depth of cut in mm
- \( v \) is the cutting speed in mm/min

$$ \text{MRR} = 60 \, \text{mm} \times 3 \, \text{mm} \times 600 \, \text{mm/min} = 108000 \, \text{mm}^3/\text{min} $$

#### 3. Cutting Time (t):
The cutting time can be calculated using the formula:
$$ t = \frac{l}{v} $$

Where:
- \( l \) is the length of cut in mm
- \( v \) is the cutting speed in mm/min

$$ t = \frac{500 \, \text{mm}}{600 \, \text{mm/min}} = 0.833 \, \text{min} $$

#### 4. Power Required (P):
The power required can be estimated using the formula:
$$ P = \frac{\text{MRR} \times \text{Specific Cutting Energy}}{60 \times 10^6} $$

Where:
- Specific Cutting Energy for high-strength aluminum alloy is approximately 0.7 J/mm³ (this value can vary based on the exact alloy and cutting conditions)

$$ P = \frac{108000 \, \text{mm}^3/\text{min} \times 0.7 \, \text{J/mm}^3}{60 \times 10^6} $$

$$ P = \frac{75600 \, \text{J/min}}{60 \times 10^6} $$

$$ P = 1.26 \, \text{kW} $$

### Summary:
- Feed per tooth (\( f_z \)) = 0.06 mm/tooth
- Metal Removal Rate (MRR) = 108000 mm³/min
- Cutting Time (t) = 0.833 min
- Power Required (P) = 1.26 kW
