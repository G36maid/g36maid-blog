### 98
Let A, B, and C be matrices 
such that (AB)C and A(BC) are defined.


we can see (AB)C = A(BC). 

Recall that function composition is associative.
(See Appendix B.) 
Using Theorem 2.12, we have

$T_{(AB)C}$ = $T_{AB}T_c$ = $(T_AT_B)T_C$
= $T_A(T_BT_C)$ = $T_A (T_{BC})$
= $T_{A(BC)}$.
Therefore the uniqueness statement in Theorem 2.9
implies that (AB)C = A(BC)

### 80

If E is obtained by performing an interchange operation on In, the result follows from Theorem 3.3(a). Let E be the elementary matrix obtained by multiplying row r of In by k. Then EA is obtained by multiplying row r of A by k, and so Theorem 3.3(b) implies that
