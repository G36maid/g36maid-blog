## 3. Tools for modern astronomy 2
 a) What are the reflecting telescope and refracting telescope ?
 (NO NEED to explain 'good and bad' of each type of
telescopes)

Reflecting Telescope: A reflecting telescope is a type of telescope that uses mirrors to gather and focus light. It consists of a curved mirror at the bottom of the telescope, called the primary mirror, which collects incoming light and reflects it towards a smaller mirror, called the secondary mirror. The secondary mirror then reflects the light out through an opening in the side of the telescope, where it is captured by the eyepiece or a camera. The curved shape of the mirrors helps to gather and focus the light, allowing astronomers to observe distant objects in space.

Refracting Telescope: A refracting telescope is another type of telescope that uses lenses to gather and focus light. It consists of a large lens at the front of the telescope, called the objective lens, which gathers incoming light and bends it towards a smaller lens, called the eyepiece lens. The eyepiece lens then magnifies the focused image, allowing us to see it more clearly. The objective lens is usually made of glass and has a curved shape that refracts or bends the light as it passes through, while the eyepiece lens helps to further magnify the image.
