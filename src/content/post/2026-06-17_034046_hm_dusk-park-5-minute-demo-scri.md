### [0:15 – 0:35] Concept & Goal

*[Screen: Click "Enter the Park." The loader fades. You stand at the edge of the plaza, sunset and fountain ahead.]*

> "Dusk Park is a 3D theme park that serves as a gateway to four games. Instead of picking a game from a menu, you **walk around a park at sunset** and step into each attraction.
>
> Your goal is simple: play the arcade games to earn tokens, then spend those tokens to ride the roller coaster. Ride it three times and you complete the tour."

### [0:35 – 1:05] The Hub — Atmosphere

*[Screen: Walk slowly toward the fountain. Look left at the Arcade Hall, right at the Coaster Station. Let the warm lamp glow and long shadows play. Press **C** for third person briefly to show the whole plaza, then back to first person.]*

> "The park sits in that brief moment between day and night — the golden hour. Everything here is bathed in warm orange light from a low sun, with street lamps glowing along the plaza.
>
> At the center is a fountain. Around it, three destinations: the **Arcade Hall** to my left, the **Coaster Station** to my right, and a free tour train behind me.
>
> Let's start by earning some tokens."

### [1:05 – 1:45] 3D Pinball

*[Screen: Walk to the Arcade Hall. Prompt appears: "Press E." Press E. The arcade menu opens showing three machines. Click "3D Pinball." Fade into the pinball scene.]*

> "Inside the Arcade Hall, I can choose from three machines. First up — **3D Pinball**."

*[Screen: ~15–20 seconds of pinball gameplay. Launch the ball, hit a few bumpers, let the flippers work. Show the neon look and the ball rolling.]*

> "This is a full pinball machine in 3D. You launch the ball, work the flippers, and chase a high score. The neon styling fits the arcade atmosphere perfectly."

*[Screen: Let the ball drain, or play until game over. The payout toast appears: "+ 10 Tokens." Then click "Return to Park." Fade back to hub. HUD updates.]* ✂️

> "Every game you finish pays out ten tokens. That goes into a shared wallet — so now I have tokens to spend."

*[Screen: Back in the hub, walk to the Arcade Hall again. Press E. This time click "Rubik's Cube." Fade in.]*

### [2:25 – 3:05] 3D Tetris

> "That's ride number one. Two more to complete the tour."

### [4:10 – 4:40] Proudest Design Choice

*[Screen: Standing in the hub, facing the fountain and sunset. Hold a steady wide shot.]*

>
> I had four separate games — each built as its own standalone project. Instead of just listing them on a menu screen, I built a world that connects them. You walk through a park, you see the attractions ahead of you, and stepping into a building *is* launching a game.
>
> The token economy ties it all together: it gives you a reason to try every game, and a reward that feels earned. The park isn't just a launcher — it's a game of its own."

*[Screen: Stay on the wide hub shot, or let the camera slowly pan across the plaza one last time.]*

## Recording Notes

**Pre-setup before recording:**
- Set `localStorage['dusk-park-state']` to give yourself enough tokens so you don't have to fully complete each arcade game live. Example: `{"tokens":0,"coasterRides":0,"gamesPlayed":{"pinball":0,"rubiks":0,"tetris":0},"achievements":[]}` — start fresh so the progression feels real, but don't worry about winning pinball; just play until game over.
- For Rubik's Cube: solving live is impractical. Record yourself scrambling it and making a few moves, then cut to a solved state (or pre-solve it in a separate session and record the payout moment).
- For the coaster: pre-record a clean ride clip so you have a fallback if the live ride stutters.

**Pacing tips:**
- The three arcade segments are the most likely to run long. Keep each gameplay clip to **15–20 seconds** — enough to show what the game is, not enough to get absorbed in playing it.
- Speak **over** the fade transitions — don't wait for them to finish before talking.
- The scissor marks are natural cut points. If a segment runs long in editing, trim there.

**Tone:** Conversational, confident, a little warm — you're showing someone around a place you built. Not a lecture.
