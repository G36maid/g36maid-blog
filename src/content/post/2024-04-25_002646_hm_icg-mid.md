## RT
3-107
1. **Primary Rays**: These rays originate from the camera or the viewer's eye and are cast into the scene. Each primary ray represents a line of sight from the viewer through a pixel in the image plane.

2. **Shadow Rays**: Shadow rays are secondary rays cast from the intersection point of a primary ray with a surface toward a light source. They are used to determine if a point on a surface is in shadow or illuminated by a light source. If the shadow ray intersects with any object before reaching the light source, the point is in shadow, otherwise, it is illuminated.

3. **Reflection Rays**: When a primary ray intersects with a reflective surface, reflection rays are generated. These rays simulate the reflection of light off the surface according to the law of reflection. Reflection rays are traced recursively to capture multiple reflections within the scene.
