### 1. Taiwan Power Company: Real-time Information on Power Generation of Each Unit (Including Purchased Power)

**URL:** [Taiwan Power Company Open Data - Power Generation](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/8931)

**Overview of the dataset:**
This dataset provides real-time information on the power generation of each unit, including purchased power. It includes details about the generation capacity, output, and types of power generation units (thermal, nuclear, hydro, renewable).

**Why the dataset can be useful for your group project:**
- **Real-time Monitoring:** Essential for tracking the current status of power generation and ensuring timely coordination.
- **Diverse Energy Sources:** Includes data from various generation sources, aiding in analyzing the energy mix and reliability.
- **Unit-specific Data:** Allows for detailed analysis of the performance and output of individual power units.

**Quality of the dataset:**
- **Timeliness:** Provides up-to-date, real-time data, crucial for accurate monitoring and analysis.
- **Detail:** Comprehensive data on each power generation unit ensures a granular level of detail.
- **Reliability:** As it comes from the official power company, the data is trustworthy.

**How this dataset could be processed/analyzed/visualized:**
- **Real-time Dashboards:** Creating dashboards to visualize real-time power generation data and monitor performance.
- **Trend Analysis:** Analyzing trends in power generation over time to identify patterns and anomalies.
- **Performance Metrics:** Calculating performance metrics for each unit to assess efficiency and reliability.
- **Energy Mix Visualization:** Visualizing the contribution of different energy sources to the overall power generation.



### 2. Taiwan Power Company: Residential, Service Industry, Government Agency, and Industrial Electricity Consumption Ratio by County and City for the Year

**URL:** [Taiwan Power Company Open Data - Consumption Ratio](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/38959)

**Overview of the dataset:**
This dataset provides the electricity consumption ratio for residential, service industry, government agency, and industrial sectors by county and city for the year. It includes detailed consumption statistics across different regions and sectors.

**Why the dataset can be useful for your group project:**
- **Sectoral Breakdown:** Allows for detailed analysis of electricity consumption across different sectors, which is crucial for targeted interventions.
- **Regional Data:** Provides insights into regional variations in electricity consumption, helping identify high and low consumption areas.
- **Yearly Data:** Enables the analysis of yearly trends and changes in electricity consumption patterns.

**Quality of the dataset:**
- **Comprehensive:** Includes detailed data on electricity consumption across various sectors and regions.
- **Accurate:** Sourced from the national power company, ensuring high accuracy and reliability.
- **Up-to-date:** Reflects the latest available data, making it relevant for current analysis.

**How this dataset could be processed/analyzed/visualized:**
- **Consumption Heatmaps:** Creating heatmaps to visualize electricity consumption patterns across different regions.
- **Sectoral Analysis:** Analyzing consumption data by sector to identify key areas of high and low usage.
- **Trend Analysis:** Examining yearly trends in electricity consumption to understand changes over time.
- **Forecasting:** Using historical consumption data to forecast future electricity demand and plan accordingly.

### Why Both Datasets Together?

- **Balance Supply and Demand:** By analyzing both datasets, you can ensure that the electricity supply meets the consumption demand, preventing shortages or overproduction.
- **Improves Coordination:** Helps in coordinating between different power plants and sectors to optimize the overall power system.
