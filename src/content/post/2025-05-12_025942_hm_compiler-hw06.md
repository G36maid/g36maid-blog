## 1. Detailed Parsing Process

Given the Grammar G1 and CFSM's Action and Goto tables, the sentence to parse is:

```
ID + (ID + ID) $
```

### Grammar G1

```
Start -> E $
E    -> E + E
E    -> ID
```

### Parsing Steps

| Step | Stack              | Input              | Action                                       |
| ---- | ------------------ | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | `[ ]`              | `ID + (ID + ID) $` | Apply `E -> ID`, replace `ID` with `E`.      |
| 2    | `[E]`              | `+ (ID + ID) $`    | Apply `E -> E + E`, shift.                   |
| 3    | `[E + E]`          | `(ID + ID) $`      | Shift on encountering `(`.                   |
| 4    | `[E + E ( ]`       | `ID + ID) $`       | Apply `E -> ID`, replace `ID` with `E`.      |
| 5    | `[E + E ( E ]`     | `+ ID) $`          | Shift on encountering `+`.                   |
| 6    | `[E + E ( E + ]`   | `ID) $`            | Apply `E -> ID`, replace `ID` with `E`.      |
| 7    | `[E + E ( E + E ]` | `) $`              | Reduce by `E -> E + E` (inside parentheses). |
| 8    | `[E + E]`          | `$`                | Reduce by `E -> E + E` (outer `E + E`).      |
| 9    | `[E]`              | `$`                | Parsing completed.                           |

## 2. Derivation Graph

The derivation steps are as follows:

```
1. Start → E $
2. E → E + E               (Apply rule E → E + E)
3. E → ID                   (Apply rule E → ID)
4. E → E + E                (Handling E + E inside parentheses)
5. E → ID                   (Apply rule E → ID)
6. E → E + E                (Apply rule E → E + E)
7. E → ID                   (Apply rule E → ID)
```

### Derivation Process:

1. `Start → E $`
2. `E → E + E`
3. `E → ID`
4. `E → E + E`
5. `E → ID`
6. `E → E + E`
7. `E → ID`
